A credit freeze stops new lenders from reading your credit report, so nobody can open new credit in your name, [including you](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts), until you lift it. In the US it is [free by federal law](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2018/09/starting-today-new-federal-law-allows-consumers-place-free-credit-freezes-yearlong-fraud-alerts) to place, lift and remove, and it [doesn't affect your credit score](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts). You place it separately at each of the three nationwide bureaus: Equifax, Experian and TransUnion.

---

## What a freeze does, and what it doesn't

In the law, a security freeze (the legal name for a credit freeze) is a restriction that stops a bureau [disclosing your report](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1) to anyone who asks for it. Lenders typically won't open an account without checking your report, so a freeze [prevents you or others from opening accounts in your name](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/).

What it doesn't do:

- **It doesn't delete anything.** The bureau still holds your file. The freeze limits who can [read it](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1).
- **It doesn't touch accounts you already have.** You can keep using your cards, and companies you already do business with can still check your report for [that account](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq). If a card number leaks, a freeze won't stop charges on it.
- **It doesn't hurt your score.** The CFPB says freezes "do not impact your credit scores [in any way whatsoever](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/)."
- **It doesn't block every check.** The federal freeze rules [don't apply](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/) to checks for employment, tenant screening or insurance. Agencies acting on a court order, warrant or subpoena, child support agencies and credit monitoring you signed up for also [get through](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1).
- **It doesn't stop prescreened credit offers.** TransUnion says a freeze [does not stop firm offers of credit](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq). To cut those, opt out at [OptOutPrescreen](/directory/opt-out-prescreen).
- **You can still see your own reports.** You can still [request and review your own reports](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/) while frozen.

## Freeze, fraud alert or lock?

| | Credit freeze | Fraud alert | Credit lock |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it does | Blocks access to your report for new credit | Asks lenders to [verify it's you](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts) before opening an account. They can still see your report | Blocks access in [a similar way](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2018/09/free-credit-freezes-are-here) |
| Cost | Free | Free | Often a fee, or bundled with paid services ([CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/)) |
| How long | Until you lift it | 1 year, renewable. 7 years (extended alert) if you've filed an identity theft report ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts)) | Set by the product |
| Where | Each bureau separately | One bureau, which must notify the other two ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts)) | Each bureau's own product |
| Guaranteed by federal law | Yes | Yes | No |

The FTC's advice: if you want a free freeze guaranteed by federal law, [choose a freeze over a lock](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2018/09/free-credit-freezes-are-here). The CFPB says locks are [no more effective](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/) than freezes. If you already have a TransUnion lock, you can [replace it with a free freeze](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq) in TransUnion's Service Center.

## Free by federal law since September 2018

Freezes became free everywhere in the US on [September 21, 2018](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2018/09/starting-today-new-federal-law-allows-consumers-place-free-credit-freezes-yearlong-fraud-alerts). Section 301 of the [Economic Growth, Regulatory Relief, and Consumer Protection Act](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-115publ174/html/PLAW-115publ174.htm) (Public Law 115-174) added the national freeze rules to the Fair Credit Reporting Act, now at [15 U.S.C. § 1681c-1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1). Before then, people in some states [paid fees](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2018/09/starting-today-new-federal-law-allows-consumers-place-free-credit-freezes-yearlong-fraud-alerts) to freeze. The same law [stretched an initial fraud alert](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2018/09/starting-today-new-federal-law-allows-consumers-place-free-credit-freezes-yearlong-fraud-alerts) from 90 days to one year.

The law sets these deadlines for the three nationwide bureaus ([15 U.S.C. § 1681c-1(i)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1)):

| Your request | Online or by phone | By mail |
|---|---|---|
| Place a freeze | Within 1 business day | Within 3 business days |
| Lift or remove it | Within 1 hour | Within 3 business days |

Placing, lifting and removing are all [free](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1). Within five business days of placing a freeze, the bureau has to [send you a confirmation](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1) and explain how to remove it. If a bureau doesn't place a freeze properly, you can [complain to the CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/) online or at 855-411-2372 ([FTC and CFPB](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2018/09/free-credit-freezes-are-here)).

## How to freeze at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion

Freeze all three. A freeze at one bureau [isn't shared](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/) with the others. Expect to give your name, address, date of birth and Social Security number so the bureau can [verify who you are](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq).

| Bureau | Online | Phone | Mail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equifax | [Security freeze page](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/) | [(888) 298-0045](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/) | Equifax Information Services LLC, P.O. Box 105788, Atlanta, GA 30348-5788 ([Equifax](https://assets.equifax.com/assets/personal/Security_Freeze_Request_Form.pdf)) |
| Experian | [Credit freeze page](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/) | [1-888-397-3742](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/) | Experian Security Freeze, P.O. Box 9554, Allen, TX 75013 ([Experian](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/)) |
| TransUnion | [Credit freeze page](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze) | [800-916-8800](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq) | TransUnion, P.O. Box 160, Woodlyn, PA 19094 ([TransUnion](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq)) |

**Online accounts replace PINs.** Online, each bureau has you create an account: a myEquifax account (you must be [18 or older](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/)), a free Experian account, or a TransUnion Service Center account. Experian and TransUnion both say you [no longer need a PIN](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/) to manage a freeze ([TransUnion](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq)). By phone, Equifax can text you a [one-time code](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/) or ask questions from your report. Save each login in a password manager such as [Bitwarden](/directory/bitwarden).

**Rather not open three more accounts?** Phone and mail work at all three, and the law's deadlines cover them. For a mailed request, Experian asks for your full name, Social Security number, addresses for the past two years, date of birth, a copy of a government ID and a copy of a utility bill or bank statement ([Experian](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/)). Send [photocopies only](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq), never originals.

**Skip the upsells.** The bureaus' freeze pages also advertise paid monitoring and lock products ([Equifax](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/), [Experian](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/)). A freeze needs none of them.

## Lifting the freeze when you apply for credit

Before you apply for a card, loan or mortgage, lift the freeze. If the lender can't see your report, the law lets it [treat your application as incomplete](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1). The FTC also suggests a temporary lift when you [apply for a job, rent an apartment or buy insurance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts), even though those checks are exempt from the freeze rules. TransUnion says landlords and employers may still [ask you to unfreeze](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq), and suggests asking the company pulling your report.

1. **Ask which bureau they use.** Lift only that one. If they can't say, lift all three.
2. **Lift it for set dates.** Experian lets you [schedule a thaw](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/) with a start and end date. TransUnion lets you [enter the dates](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq) and schedule up to 15 days ahead. Equifax lets you [temporarily lift or remove](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/) the freeze from your myEquifax account.
3. **Give it a moment.** An online or phone lift has to happen within an hour by law. TransUnion suggests waiting [a few minutes](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq) before the lender pulls your report.
4. **Let it close.** A temporary lift runs only for the [period you choose](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1). If you removed the freeze completely, place it again when you're done.

## The optional extra bureaus

Beyond the big three, smaller consumer reporting companies build reports for [particular industries](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/), such as banking or utilities. The CFPB keeps a [list of them](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/). Freezing these three is optional. Each publishes its own fees and timing, and a lift can take [days, not an hour](https://www.innovis.com/personal/lc_securityFreeze).

- **Innovis** provides credit and identity verification data ([CFPB list](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/)). Freeze [online, by phone at 1-866-712-4546, or by mail](https://www.innovis.com/personal/securityFreeze). Placing, lifting and removing are [free](https://www.innovis.com/personal/lc_securityFreeze). Innovis mails you a 10-digit PIN to manage the freeze, so keep it. Freezes are typically added within three business days, and most state laws give Innovis three business days to lift one ([Innovis](https://www.innovis.com/personal/lc_securityFreeze)). Lift it well before you apply.
- **ChexSystems** collects data on checking account applications, openings and closures ([CFPB list](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/)). Its freeze is designed to stop [checking, savings and credit accounts](https://www.chexsystems.com/security-freeze/information) being approved in your name without your consent. Place it through its [Consumer Portal, by phone at 800-887-7652, or by mail](https://www.chexsystems.com/security-freeze/place-freeze). ChexSystems [doesn't charge](https://www.chexsystems.com/security-freeze/notice-of-rights) to place or lift a freeze, but keeps the right to charge where state law allows. You get a PIN by mail or in the portal. Lift it well before you open a new bank account.
- **NCTUE**, the National Consumer Telecom & Utilities Exchange, holds phone, pay TV and utility account histories ([CFPB list](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/)). Equifax [manages the data](https://nctue.com/consumer/) for its members. Freezing, lifting and removing are [free](https://nctue.com/consumer/), through the consumer portal linked from that page.

## Freezing for children and dependents

- **Under 16.** A parent or guardian can request a [free freeze](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts). Children generally don't have credit reports unless someone is [misusing their information](https://www.identitytheft.gov/databreach). If a bureau has no file, the law has it [create a record and freeze it](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/). That record can't be used for credit.
- **16 or 17.** Teens this age can [request and remove a freeze themselves](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-protect-your-child-identity-theft).
- **Adults in your care.** Guardians, conservators and people with a valid power of attorney can get a [free freeze](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2018/09/starting-today-new-federal-law-allows-consumers-place-free-credit-freezes-yearlong-fraud-alerts) for the person they act for. The law's [protected consumers](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1) are children under 16 and "an incapacitated person or a protected person for whom a guardian or conservator has been appointed." A valid power of attorney is one of the papers the law accepts as proof you can act for them.

**What to send.** Expect to provide your ID, proof of your address, and the child's birth certificate and Social Security card. A guardian who isn't a parent may also need papers showing legal guardianship ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-protect-your-child-identity-theft)).

**Where to send it.** Each bureau has its own process:

- Equifax: the [Minor Freeze Request form](https://assets.equifax.com/assets/personal/Minor_Freeze_Request_Form.pdf), or the [Incapacitated Adult Freeze Request form](https://assets.equifax.com/assets/personal/Incapacitated_Adult_Freeze_Request_Form.pdf) ([Equifax](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/)).
- Experian: its [minor request page](https://www.experian.com/help/minor-request/) for a child. For an adult in your care, Equifax says to [contact Experian directly](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/), at [1-888-397-3742](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/).
- TransUnion: by mail only, to TransUnion, P.O. Box 380, Woodlyn, PA 19094 ([TransUnion](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq)).

A child's freeze [stays in place](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-protect-your-child-identity-theft) until you ask the bureaus to remove it. Lift it before your child applies for a first card, car loan or student loan. Signs that someone is already using a child's identity include calls about overdue bills for accounts you never opened, or a student loan [denied for bad credit](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-protect-your-child-identity-theft).

## If your data was in a breach

Most of these steps follow IdentityTheft.gov's [data breach guide](https://www.identitytheft.gov/databreach), run by the FTC.

1. **Freeze at all three bureaus.** IdentityTheft.gov puts checking, freezing and monitoring your credit at step one, whatever was exposed.
2. **Check your credit reports.** You can get each bureau's report [free every week](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/free-credit-reports) at [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com/), the only authorized site for the free reports. Look for accounts or debts you don't recognize.
3. **If someone used your information,** report it at [IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/) to get a recovery plan. That report also qualifies you for a [7-year extended fraud alert](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts).
4. **If your Social Security number was exposed,** get an IRS [Identity Protection PIN](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin). It's a six-digit number that stops anyone else filing a tax return with your Social Security number, and parents can request one for dependents. The PIN [changes every year](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin). If you sign up online, no letter comes, so look up the new one online each year. A missing or wrong PIN gets an e-filed return rejected. You can also lock your number in E-Verify with [Self Lock](https://www.e-verify.gov/employees/employee-self-services/mye-verify/self-lock), which makes it harder for someone to use it to get a job with an E-Verify employer. Unlock it before you start a new job, or your new employer's check will come back as a [mismatch](https://www.e-verify.gov/employees/employee-self-services/mye-verify/self-lock).
5. **If a password was exposed,** change it, and change it anywhere you reused it. Turn on two-factor login ([IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/databreach)). Use our [breach check](/breach-check), which sends you to Have I Been Pwned, to see which other services have leaked your email address, and let a password manager such as [Bitwarden](/directory/bitwarden) generate a new password for each site.
6. **If a card or bank account number was exposed,** call your bank or card company to cancel the card or close the account and get a new one ([IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/databreach)). A freeze doesn't cover accounts you already have, so this step matters.
7. **If the breached company offers free credit monitoring,** IdentityTheft.gov says to [accept it](https://www.identitytheft.gov/databreach). Nothing on this list requires paying for anything.

## FAQ

**Does freezing my credit hurt my credit score?**

No. A freeze doesn't affect your credit score ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts); [CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/)).

**Can I still use my credit cards while my credit is frozen?**

Yes. A freeze has no effect on cards you already have ([TransUnion](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq)). It only blocks new accounts that need a credit check.

**How long does it take to unfreeze my credit?**

At Equifax, Experian and TransUnion, a lift requested online or by phone must take effect within one hour, and a mailed request within three business days of arrival ([15 U.S.C. § 1681c-1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c-1)). Innovis says most state laws give it three business days ([Innovis](https://www.innovis.com/personal/lc_securityFreeze)).

**Do I have to freeze at all three bureaus?**

Yes. Each bureau's freeze covers only its own report, and a freeze at one [isn't passed to the others](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/).

**Is a credit lock the same as a credit freeze?**

No. They work in a similar way, but a lock may carry monthly fees, and only a freeze is free and [guaranteed by federal law](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2018/09/free-credit-freezes-are-here). The CFPB says locks are [no more effective](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/).

**Will a freeze stop junk credit card offers?**

No. A freeze [doesn't stop prescreened offers](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze/credit-freeze-faq). Opt out of those separately at [OptOutPrescreen](/directory/opt-out-prescreen).

**Should I freeze my child's credit?**

If your child is under 16, the FTC says to request a [free freeze](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-protect-your-child-identity-theft) to make it harder for someone to open accounts in their name. Most bureaus handle it by mail or a separate form. Remember to lift it before your child applies for credit.

**How do I stop someone filing taxes in my name?**

The IRS runs its own protection. An [Identity Protection PIN](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin) stops anyone else filing a return with your Social Security number. It changes every year, and a missing or wrong PIN gets an e-filed return rejected ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin)).
