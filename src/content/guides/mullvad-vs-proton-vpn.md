**Last checked: October 2026** · Each claim links to Mullvad's or Proton's own pages, a published audit, or Proton's transparency report. Prices and features change, so check the linked page before you pay.

Pick Mullvad if you want to sign up and pay without giving a name or email address: it issues a random account number, takes cash by post and charges a flat €5 a month. Pick Proton VPN if you want a free plan, streaming, port forwarding or one account shared with Proton Mail. Both publish their app code, both have had their servers examined by outside auditors, and both say they keep no activity logs.

"Mullvad or Proton VPN?" was among the most-asked comparisons on the former Techlore privacy forum. Here is how the two compare as of October 2026.

## Signing up

**Mullvad** asks for no username, password or email address. It generates a random account number instead ([no-logging policy](https://mullvad.net/en/help/no-logging-data-policy)), and you choose how to pay once you have it ([pricing](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing)). Store the number somewhere safe, such as a password manager.

**Proton VPN** uses a Proton account. If you already use Proton Mail or Proton Drive, the same username and password sign you in ([Android guide](https://protonvpn.com/support/best-android-vpn-app)). Proton's sign-up guide says signing up through Proton VPN's own page asks for an email address from another provider, which becomes your username and recovery address ([sign-up guide](https://protonvpn.com/support/how-to-create-free-vpn-account)). A Proton Mail account works too, and Proton's privacy policy says no personal information is needed to create an account ([privacy policy](https://proton.me/legal/privacy)).

Either way, Proton may ask you to prove you're human with a CAPTCHA, an email code or a text message. It says it keeps only a cryptographic hash of the email address or phone number you use for that ([human verification](https://proton.me/support/human-verification)). On Android and iOS you can skip the account and use the free features as a guest ([Android](https://protonvpn.com/support/best-android-vpn-app), [iOS](https://protonvpn.com/support/protonvpn-ios-vpn-app)).

## Paying

**Mullvad** accepts cash, Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash, Monero, bank wire, credit card, PayPal, Swish and several European bank transfer methods. Cryptocurrency payments get 10% off ([pricing](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing)). To pay cash, you generate a payment token on the website and post it with the money. It takes cash in nine currencies, including euros, US dollars and pounds ([pricing](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing)). It says it adds the time to your account, then shreds the envelope and everything in it except the money ([no-logging policy](https://mullvad.net/en/help/no-logging-data-policy)).

Other methods leave a record. For card payments Mullvad keeps the last four digits, card type and country. For PayPal it keeps the sender's name, country and email address. A bank wire leaves your name, address and account number, and Swish your name and phone number. Mullvad says Swedish accounting law requires some payment data to be kept for seven years from the end of the financial year ([privacy policy](https://mullvad.net/en/help/privacy-policy), July 2025).

**Proton VPN** accepts cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Bitcoin, cash and bank transfer. Cash goes by post to Proton's office in Geneva, in Swiss francs, US dollars or euros, and must include your Proton Mail username so Proton knows which account to credit. Bitcoin doesn't appear when you first sign up: you create a free account, then upgrade from its settings ([payment options](https://protonvpn.com/support/payment-options)).

Neither company refunds cash payments ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing), [Proton](https://protonvpn.com/support/payment-options)). Mullvad cites anti-money-laundering rules.

## Price

**Mullvad** costs €5 a month including VAT, whether you pay for one month or ten years, and says the price hasn't changed since it launched in 2009 ([pricing](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing)). There is no free plan.

**Proton VPN** has a free plan and paid ones. The US prices on its pricing page in October 2026 ([pricing](https://protonvpn.com/pricing)):

- **Free:** $0, 1 device, "medium" speed, servers in 10 countries, "randomly selected".
- **VPN Plus:** $9.99 a month on monthly billing. The two-year plan works out to $3.49 a month, billed $83.76 up front, then renews at $83.88 a year. 10 devices.
- **Proton Unlimited:** VPN Plus plus Proton Mail, Pass, Calendar and Drive, $7.99 a month on the two-year plan, then $119.88 a year.

Over two years, Mullvad costs €120, about $134 at the $5.60 a month Mullvad lists as the equivalent ([pricing](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing)). Proton's two-year VPN Plus plan costs $83.76, then renews at $83.88 a year ([pricing](https://protonvpn.com/pricing)).

## Logs, audits and legal requests

### Mullvad

Mullvad says it logs no traffic, no DNS requests, no connection times, no IP addresses and no bandwidth use. It checks connections in temporary memory only ([no-logging policy](https://mullvad.net/en/help/no-logging-data-policy), updated June 2026). Since September 2023 its VPN servers have run entirely from RAM, with no disks ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/we-have-successfully-completed-our-migration-to-ram-only-vpn-infrastructure)).

Outside audits of its servers:

- **Radically Open Security, 2023:** examined two RAM-only VPN servers and "found no logging of user activity data". It also reported 25 findings, one rated high ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/infrastructure-audit-completed-by-radically-open-security)).
- **Cure53, June 2024:** tested two servers in Mullvad's staging setup, which is configured like production but carries no customers. It found one medium and one low-severity issue, both fixed ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/fourth-infrastructure-audit-completed-by-cure53), [report](https://cure53.de/pentest-report_mullvad_2024_v1.pdf)).

Mullvad said in 2024 that it would audit its VPN servers again in 2025 ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/fourth-infrastructure-audit-completed-by-cure53)). Its audit list in October 2026 shows newer audits of its account and payment system, its WireGuard code and its Android app, but no newer server audit ([audit list](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/tag/audits)).

The one known police visit: on April 18, 2023, Swedish police arrived with a search warrant to seize computers holding customer data. Mullvad says that after it showed how its service works and the police consulted the prosecutor, they left without taking anything ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/mullvad-vpn-was-subject-to-a-search-warrant-customer-data-not-compromised)).

### Proton VPN

Proton says it does not log users' traffic or VPN session data, and that the free plan is covered by the same no-logs policy ([privacy policy](https://protonvpn.com/privacy-policy), July 2025). Its servers use full-disk encryption ([privacy policy](https://protonvpn.com/privacy-policy)).

Securitum has audited the no-logs policy every year since 2022. Proton says the auditors spent several days on site each year reviewing server configurations and procedures and interviewing staff. The 2026 report found nothing to indicate that the servers it examined log "browsing activity, DNS queries, destination services, network traffic contents or user-identifiable connection metadata" ([Proton](https://protonvpn.com/blog/no-logs-audit), where all five reports are linked).

Proton's transparency report says Swiss law doesn't oblige Proton VPN to keep activity logs. It received 59 legal orders for VPN data in 2025 and denied all of them. Every VPN request so far has asked who was connected to a given server at a given time, which Proton says it cannot answer ([transparency report](https://proton.me/legal/transparency), January 2026).

Proton Mail is treated differently. In 2025 Proton complied with 8,313 legal orders for Proton Mail ([transparency report](https://proton.me/legal/transparency)). In 2021, after complying with a binding Swiss order about a Proton Mail account, Proton explained that Swiss law can force it to collect information on the accounts of users under Swiss criminal investigation. The same post says "email and VPN are treated differently, and Proton VPN cannot be compelled to log user data" ([Proton, September 2021](https://proton.me/blog/climate-activist-arrest)). If you'd rather your VPN not sit on the same account as your email, use a separate account.

## Who owns them, and where

**Mullvad** is Mullvad VPN AB of Gothenburg, Sweden. Its parent, Amagicom AB, is wholly owned by its two founders ([about](https://mullvad.net/en/about)).

**Proton VPN** is run by Proton AG in Geneva. Since June 2024 its main shareholder has been the Proton Foundation, a Swiss non-profit ([Proton](https://proton.me/blog/proton-non-profit-foundation)). In July 2025, citing legal uncertainty around Swiss government surveillance proposals, Proton said it was moving most of its physical infrastructure out of Switzerland ([Proton](https://proton.me/blog/lumo-ai)). Its VPN privacy policy says account data is stored on servers in Switzerland, Germany or Norway ([privacy policy](https://protonvpn.com/privacy-policy)).

## Features that differ

- **Streaming.** Mullvad says streaming services often block VPNs, and its guide shows how to exclude Netflix from the VPN with split tunneling ([help](https://mullvad.net/en/help/watching-netflix-with-split-tunneling)). Split tunneling is in Mullvad's apps for Windows, Linux, macOS and Android. It isn't available on iOS ([split tunneling](https://mullvad.net/en/help/split-tunneling-with-the-mullvad-app)). Proton supports streaming on paid plans and asks you to use a legal account with the service ([streaming guide](https://protonvpn.com/support/streaming-guide)).
- **Port forwarding.** Mullvad removed it because people used it to host "undesirable content and malicious services", which got its IP addresses blacklisted and its hosting providers cancelling it ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/removing-the-support-for-forwarded-ports)). Proton offers it on paid plans ([port forwarding](https://protonvpn.com/support/port-forwarding)).
- **Getting past VPN blocks.** Mullvad's apps include obfuscation methods such as UDP-over-TCP ([app guide](https://mullvad.net/en/help/using-mullvad-vpn-app)). Proton's Stealth protocol is available on every plan, including Free ([Proton, 2022](https://protonvpn.com/blog/stealth-vpn-protocol)).
- **Traffic analysis.** Mullvad's DAITA is a countermeasure against traffic analysis that could identify which websites you visit ([Android guide](https://mullvad.net/en/help/using-mullvad-vpn-on-android)).
- **Protocols.** Mullvad said OpenVPN would stop working on its service on January 15, 2026, leaving WireGuard only ([Mullvad, November 2024](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/removing-openvpn-15th-january-2026), [final reminder, December 2025](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/final-reminder-for-openvpn-removal)). A router or third-party app set up for Mullvad's OpenVPN needs a WireGuard setup instead.

## What neither one changes

Proton puts it plainly: "Your VPN provider knows your real IP address, resolves your DNS queries, can see which websites you visit, and likely has your payment details." A VPN also can't stop tracking by cookies, browser fingerprinting or the accounts you sign in to ([Proton](https://protonvpn.com/blog/can-be-tracked-using-vpn)). Choosing between these two is choosing whom you trust with that view.

- To check your setup, connect and run our [DNS leak test](/dns-leak).
- For a browser built to resist fingerprinting, [Mullvad Browser](/directory/mullvad-browser) is free, made with the Tor Project and works with or without a VPN ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/browser)).
- If you need to hide what you do from the VPN company as well, look at [Tor Browser](/directory/tor-browser).
- If you only want to keep your traffic away from your internet provider or public Wi-Fi, you can [run your own VPN with WireGuard](/guides/run-your-own-vpn-wireguard).

**Pick [Mullvad](/directory/mullvad) if you:**

- want to pay with Monero, or get 10% off for paying in cryptocurrency ([pricing](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing))
- want one price with no renewal increase
- don't need streaming or port forwarding

**Pick [Proton VPN](/directory/protonvpn) if you:**

- are happy to prepay two years for a lower price

## FAQ

**Is Mullvad or Proton VPN more private?**

Mullvad collects less to start with: no email, no username, and cash or cryptocurrency payment if you want it ([no-logging policy](https://mullvad.net/en/help/no-logging-data-policy), [pricing](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing)). On activity logs the two make the same promise, and both have had it examined by outside auditors ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/infrastructure-audit-completed-by-radically-open-security), [Proton](https://protonvpn.com/blog/no-logs-audit)).

**Is Proton VPN's free plan safe to use?**

Proton says the free plan has no ads and no data limits, is paid for by paying customers, and is covered by the same no-logs policy ([free plan](https://protonvpn.com/free-vpn), [privacy policy](https://protonvpn.com/privacy-policy)). Securitum's audit checks that the policy applies to every subscription tier ([Proton](https://protonvpn.com/blog/no-logs-audit)). The limits: 1 device, servers in 10 countries, no streaming and no Secure Core ([pricing](https://protonvpn.com/pricing), [Secure Core](https://protonvpn.com/support/secure-core-vpn)).

**Can police get my browsing history from Mullvad or Proton VPN?**

Both say they keep no record of it. When Swedish police came to Mullvad with a search warrant in 2023, they left without customer data ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/mullvad-vpn-was-subject-to-a-search-warrant-customer-data-not-compromised)). Proton denied all 59 legal orders for VPN data it received in 2025 ([transparency report](https://proton.me/legal/transparency)). Records they do keep, such as payment details, are a separate matter ([Mullvad privacy policy](https://mullvad.net/en/help/privacy-policy)).

**Does Mullvad work with Netflix?**

Mullvad doesn't promise it. It says Netflix frequently blocks VPNs and may show only its own original content. Its suggested fix is to exclude Netflix from the VPN with split tunneling ([help](https://mullvad.net/en/help/watching-netflix-with-split-tunneling)). Proton supports streaming on its paid plans ([streaming guide](https://protonvpn.com/support/streaming-guide)).

**Which one supports port forwarding?**

Proton VPN, on paid plans, on P2P servers, in its Windows, macOS and Linux apps, with macOS in early access ([port forwarding](https://protonvpn.com/support/port-forwarding)). Mullvad removed port forwarding on July 1, 2023 ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/removing-the-support-for-forwarded-ports)).

**Can I pay for a VPN with cash?**

Both accept cash by post. Mullvad needs a payment token from its website in the envelope ([pricing](https://mullvad.net/en/pricing)). Proton needs your Proton Mail username and takes Swiss francs, US dollars or euros ([payment options](https://protonvpn.com/support/payment-options)). Neither refunds cash payments.

**Do I need an email address for Proton VPN?**

Not necessarily. Proton's sign-up guide says Proton VPN's own sign-up page asks for one ([sign-up guide](https://protonvpn.com/support/how-to-create-free-vpn-account)), but a Proton Mail account works too, and the free features run without any account on Android and iOS ([Android](https://protonvpn.com/support/best-android-vpn-app), [iOS](https://protonvpn.com/support/protonvpn-ios-vpn-app)). Proton may still ask for an email or phone number to confirm you're human ([human verification](https://proton.me/support/human-verification)).

**Is Mullvad Browser the same as Mullvad VPN?**

No. Mullvad Browser is a separate, free browser made with the Tor Project. It works with any VPN or none ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/browser)).

**Can I use Mullvad or Proton VPN with Tails?**

Tails says it does not work with VPNs, and that a VPN gives weaker anonymity than Tor because the provider can see both where you connect from and where you connect to ([Tails FAQ](https://tails.net/support/faq/)). Some write-ups describe importing a Mullvad config file in Tails' network settings ([linuxmind](https://linuxmind.dev/2025/11/25/how-to-choose-use-and-configure-a-vpn-in-tails-the-amnesic-incognito-live-system-comparison/)). Those steps use OpenVPN files, and Mullvad removed OpenVPN on 15 January 2026, from both its app and its servers ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/removing-openvpn-15th-january-2026)). We could not verify that Tails' network settings accept a WireGuard file, so we won't give steps.
