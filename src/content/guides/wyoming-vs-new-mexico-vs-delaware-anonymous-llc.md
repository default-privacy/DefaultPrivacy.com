**Current as of October 2026 · General information, not legal advice**

All three keep your name off the **public formation record**. The right pick comes down to **cost, ongoing filings, charging-order reputation, and what you're building**.

**Cheapest? New Mexico. Best recognized privacy brand? Wyoming. Complex equity or VC? Delaware.**

Anonymity **leaks the same way in all three states**: registered agent, IRS EIN responsible party, bank KYC, litigation discovery. State choice changes cost and corporate-law features, not who can identify you with legal process. See [What an Anonymous LLC Does NOT Do](/guides/what-anonymous-llc-does-not-do).

---

## Side-by-side (state fees only)

| | Wyoming | New Mexico | Delaware |
|---|---------|------------|----------|
| **Filing cost** | ~$100 | **~$50, cheapest** | ~$110 |
| **Annual report / fee** | Annual report (no member names listed) | **None. No annual report** | **$400/yr** tax, no annual report |
| **Member names public?** | No | No | No |
| **Charging-order reputation** | Strongest brand (WY courts) | Standard | Strong case law |
| **Federal ownership report (U.S.-formed)** | None* | None* | None* |
| **Best for** | Operating companies, recognized privacy stack | Cost-sensitive holding / quiet entities | VC, complex equity, Chancery court |

\*A company formed in the United States does not file a federal beneficial-ownership report. FinCEN made that exemption permanent on August 14, 2026 ([FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/boi)). Delaware's yearly tax is $400, due June 1, with no annual report ([Delaware Division of Corporations](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/)).

Formation service fees are separate from and additional to the state filing fees shown above.

---

## Pick New Mexico if…

- You want the **lowest state cost** to form and maintain
- You'd rather **never file an annual report**
- It's a single-member **holding company** or quiet side entity
- Keeping your home address off public record is the primary goal

---

## Pick Wyoming if…

- You want the most **recognized privacy / charging-order reputation**
- You're running an **operating business** (clients, contracts, revenue)
- Banks and partners know Wyoming LLCs, so account opening is straightforward
- You may add a holding layer above it later

---

## Pick Delaware if…

- You're raising **venture capital** or expect outside investors
- You have **multiple owners** or complex equity
- You want the **Court of Chancery** and deep corporate precedent
- You're comfortable with **$400/yr** for prestige and flexibility

For most solo founders and privacy buyers, **NM (cost) or WY (brand)** wins. DE earns its premium when investors or complex equity are involved.

---

## Where "anonymous" leaks (all three states)

- **Registered agent.** Knows who you are, and can be compelled under subpoena
- **IRS EIN.** Responsible party with SSN/ITIN on file ([IRS SS-4](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4))
- **Bank KYC.** Beneficial owner identity required to open accounts
- **Courts.** Discovery, debtor's exams, and subpoenas compel disclosure

Choosing Wyoming over New Mexico does not change these.

## Questions people ask

**Which state is best for an anonymous LLC?**

No single winner. NM for cost and upkeep, WY for operating businesses and brand, DE for investor-backed structures. All three keep member names off public formation records.

**Is New Mexico really the cheapest?**

For **state** costs: about $50 to form, and no annual report. You still pay for a registered agent and you still comply with EIN and bank KYC.

**Does any state make me judgment-proof?**

No. In any state, an anonymous LLC is **business privacy**, not asset protection or creditor immunity.

**Do I still file an ownership report?**

A company formed in the United States does not. FinCEN's final rule, effective August 14, 2026, made that permanent ([FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/boi)). A company formed abroad and registered here can still have to file.
