**Last checked: October 2026** · Setting names below are what each company's own pages say, with a link and the date we read them; menus change with every update, and we could not test on every phone. For laptops and browsers, see [Turn Off the AI on Your Computer](/guides/turn-off-ai-on-your-computer).

Phone updates have switched on AI features. Some run on the phone. Some send what you type, say or see to a company's servers. For each one, this guide answers three questions:

1. **What is on?** What the update turned on, or put one tap away.
2. **Where does my data go?** On the phone, or to a company's servers, and for how long.
3. **How do I turn it off?** The exact setting, where the company documents one.

---

## If you only do three things

1. **Android: disconnect Gemini from Phone, Messages and WhatsApp.** Turning off Gemini's activity setting does not do this. Open the Gemini app, tap Menu, your profile picture, then Connected Apps, and turn each one off ([Google](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13695044?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid), read 2026-10-10).
2. **Gmail: uncheck the three "smart features" boxes.** Google says they are off by default only in the EEA, Japan, Switzerland and the UK ([Google](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/10079371?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid), read 2026-10-10). The path is in the Gmail section below.
3. **iPhone: start logging what leaves your phone.** Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Intelligence Report and set it to the last 7 days. It only records from the moment you change it, so come back in a week and tap Export Activity. It lists requests sent to Private Cloud Compute, Apple's AI servers ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-intelligence-and-privacy-iphe3f499e0e/ios), read 2026-10-10).

## iPhone: Apple Intelligence and Siri

**Apple Intelligence** is Apple's name for the AI features in iOS: writing tools, summaries, image tools and the new Siri.

### Is it on?

Yes, for most people with a recent iPhone. Since iOS 18.3 (January 2025), Apple Intelligence has been "enabled automatically during iPhone onboarding" for people setting up or upgrading ([Apple developer release notes](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/ios-ipados-release-notes/ios-ipados-18_3-release-notes.json)). For iOS 27, released in September 2026, Apple says the new version "is automatically available on eligible devices when you update" ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115), published 2026-09-14). Apple lists the iPhone 15 Pro and Pro Max, iPhone Air, and every iPhone 16 or later. It takes up to 8 GB of storage on most eligible iPhones, and up to 14 GB on the iPhone 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max and iPhone Air.

### Is there one off switch?

On iOS 26, yes. Apple's guide says: go to Settings, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri, then tap the switch next to Apple Intelligence ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/intro-to-apple-intelligence-iphc28624b81/26/ios/26)). An older version of Apple's support article said that "If you turn off Apple Intelligence, the on-device models may be removed from your device" ([archived copy, 2025-02-26](https://web.archive.org/web/20250226155237/https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)).

On iOS 27, we could not confirm it. Apple's iOS 27 article on turning off Apple Intelligence lists only separate switches for each feature, and the settings screen is now called just "Siri" ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-iph3fed3f2c3/ios), read 2026-10-10). That doesn't prove the single switch is gone. If you see one on your phone, use it. If not, work through the list below.

### The per-feature switches on iOS 27

All from Apple's iOS 27 guide ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-iph3fed3f2c3/ios)):

- **Notification summaries:** Settings > Notifications > Summarize Notifications, turn off Summarize Notifications.
- **Priority notifications:** Settings > Notifications > Prioritize Notifications, turn off. Apple says this choice "is made by an on-device model" ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/intelligence-engine/)).
- **Messages summaries:** Settings > Apps > Messages, turn off Summarize Messages.
- **Mail summaries and replies:** Settings > Apps > Mail, turn off Summarize Message Previews and Personalize Smart Replies.
- **Voicemail suggestions:** Settings > Apps > Phone, turn off Suggestions in Voicemail.
- **Call Context cards:** Settings > Apps > Phone > Apple Intelligence & Siri, turn off Show When Calling.
- **Journal:** Settings > Apps > Journal, turn off Get Writing Prompts.

Under Settings > Siri, **Highlight to Search** (Siri AI only) can look up what's in your camera view, and **Automatic Visual Look Up** identifies objects and places in your screenshots. Apple says highlighted objects can be sent to third parties ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-siri-settings-iphc28624b81abc/ios)). Apple doesn't say whether they start on or off. Check yours.

If you want a firmer block, Apple's iOS 26 guide lists Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions. Turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions, then tap Intelligence & Siri. That screen has Writing Tools, Image Creation and Intelligence Extensions, each of which you can set to Don't Allow ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-iph3fed3f2c3/26/ios/26)).

### Siri AI (Beta) and Siri Classic

The new **Siri AI** in iOS 27 is opt-in. You only get it if you go to Settings > Siri and tap "Try Siri AI (Beta)." Apple says it "can search your photos, mail, messages, calendar, and more," and can see what's on your screen ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-siri-ai-iphv6zwrg8jvfgr/ios)). Apple says it is "Available in English to start" and "will not be initially available in the EU on iOS" ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-intelligence-and-privacy-iphe3f499e0e/ios)).

Siri AI keeps your conversations in a Siri app, which can sync across your Apple devices through iCloud. To limit how long it keeps them: Settings > Siri > Keep Conversations. Apple says if you choose 30 days or 1 year, "older conversations are permanently deleted" ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/siri-ai-use-the-siri-app-p7ny364ngxlj/ios)).

If you tried it and want the old Siri back: tap Siri, scroll down, tap Turn Off Siri and confirm. Then tap Turn on Siri and choose **Siri Classic** ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-iph3fed3f2c3/ios)).

### ChatGPT inside Siri

The ChatGPT extension "is off by default" ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/chatgpt-extension/), dated 2026-09-14). If you turned it on, Apple says your request, attachments and some device details go to OpenAI, and your IP address is hidden but "your general location is provided." If you are signed in to ChatGPT, your ChatGPT account settings apply ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-on-chatgpt-iph00fd3c8c2/ios)). Apple says that means OpenAI "may log your request, attachments, and session history, and use this data to train or improve their models." If you use it signed out, Apple says OpenAI "must not use your information to improve or train its models" ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/chatgpt-extension/)).

To turn it off on iOS 27: Settings > Siri > ChatGPT, turn off Use ChatGPT. With Siri AI: Settings > Siri > Extensions > ChatGPT, turn off Use Extension. If you keep it with Siri Classic, leave **Confirm Requests** on (Settings > Siri > ChatGPT) so Siri asks before sending anything. With Siri AI that switch isn't offered, and Apple says you must ask for ChatGPT before anything is sent ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-on-chatgpt-iph00fd3c8c2/ios)).

### Improve Siri & Dictation

This is opt-in. If you turned it on, Apple collects audio of your Siri and Dictation requests, transcripts and your approximate location. Apple "may retain and use this data for up to two years," and the small part reviewed by Apple employees "may be kept beyond two years" ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/improve-siri-dictation/), dated 2026-09-14). Apple's ChatGPT page also names a setting called Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/chatgpt-extension/)). Neither page gives the path. Search your Settings for "Improve Siri."

Even without opting in, Apple may keep Siri transcripts for up to two years and review some. Apple says they are linked to a random ID that rotates and "is not tied to your Apple Account." Apple says Settings > Siri > Siri & Dictation History > Delete Siri & Dictation History deletes the transcripts "stored on your device" ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/ask-siri-dictation/)). It doesn't say this removes Apple's own copy.

### Where it runs: your phone or Apple's servers

Apple says many requests run entirely on the phone. Harder ones go to **Private Cloud Compute**, Apple's own servers, where data "is not stored or made accessible to Apple" ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-intelligence-and-privacy-iphe3f499e0e/ios)). The Apple Intelligence Report in the checklist shows which requests went there.

### Does Siri use Google's Gemini?

Partly. In a joint statement on January 12, 2026, Apple and Google said "[T]he next generation of Apple Foundation Models will be based on Google's Gemini models and cloud technology," and that "Apple Intelligence will continue to run on Apple devices and Private Cloud Compute" ([Google](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/company-announcements/joint-statement-google-apple/)). So Apple's next models are to be built on Gemini technology and run on Apple's phones and servers. Neither company says your Siri requests go to Google.

## Android and Pixel: Gemini

**Gemini** is Google's AI assistant. Google says "Some Android devices come with Gemini as the primary assistant out of the box," and others get a prompt to switch ([Google](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14554984?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid), read 2026-10-10).

To switch back, in the Gemini app tap Menu > your profile picture > Switch to Google Assistant > Google Assistant, then tap Switch. You can also pick the assistant under Settings > Apps > Default apps > Digital assistant app.

### Keep Activity: what Google keeps

**Gemini Apps Activity**, shown as **Keep Activity**, decides whether your Gemini chats are saved to your Google account.

- "If you're 18 or over, Keep Activity is on by default" ([Google](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid)).
- Chats are auto-deleted after 18 months by default. You can change that to 3 months, 36 months or never ([Google Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en), updated 2026-09-24).
- While it's on, Google uses your chats for "training generative AI models," with human reviewers. Reviewed chats "are not deleted when you delete your activity" and are kept for up to three years.
- With it off, chats are still kept with your account for 72 hours, and Google still uses them "to respond to you and help protect Google," including with human reviewers. Google says it won't use future chats to improve its AI models if you also don't send feedback.

To turn it off: Gemini app > Menu > your profile picture > Gemini Apps Activity. Near the top, tap On, then Turn off or Turn off and delete activity ([Google](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid)). On the web it's at myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.

### The part most people miss: calls and messages stay connected

Turning off Keep Activity does not disconnect Gemini from your Phone, Messages and WhatsApp apps. Google's wording: "Some Connected Apps such as Phone, Messages, WhatsApp, and Device assistance are available on your Android mobile device, whether Keep Activity is on or off" ([Google Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en), updated 2026-09-24). Google says that "When you use Connected Apps in Gemini to help you make calls or send messages," Gemini collects and processes your "Device calling and messaging logs," contact info and device settings. It also says messages you send through the Gemini app "are not end-to-end-encrypted by Google."

To disconnect them: Gemini app > Menu > your profile picture > **Connected Apps**. If you don't see it, tap Personal Intelligence first, then Connected Apps. Find Phone, Messages, WhatsApp and Device assistance, and turn each one off ([Google](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13695044?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid)).

### Gemini in Google Messages

Chats with Gemini inside Google Messages "aren't end-to-end encrypted." To hide the button: Messages > your profile picture > Messages settings > Gemini in Messages, turn off Show Gemini button ([Google](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/14599070?hl=en)).

### Pixel features that stay on the phone

Some Pixel AI features run on the phone itself:

- **Scam Detection** is off until you opt in, and Google says processing "is all done on-device," with no call audio or transcript stored or sent to Google. Turn off at Phone app > More > Settings > Scam Detection ([Google](https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/15654065?hl=en)).
- **Call Screen**'s AI replies "are generated on device," and screened calls aren't saved to your Google account. To stop automatic screening: Phone app > More > Settings > Spam and Call Screen > Call Screen, turn off Automatically screen calls ([Google](https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/9118387?hl=en)).

**Magic Cue** (Pixel 10 and later) is less clear. At launch in August 2025, Google said it runs "privately and securely on your phone" ([Google](https://blog.google/products/pixel/google-pixel-10-pro-xl/)). Google's store page now says it processes "on your device and in the cloud" and that you can "turn it off at any time" ([Google Store](https://store.google.com/intl/en/ideas/articles/magic-cue/)). The cloud part is **Private AI Compute**, Google's server system that it says keeps data "accessible only to you and no one else, not even Google" ([Google](https://blog.google/technology/ai/google-private-ai-compute/)). We could not find Google's instructions for turning Magic Cue off in Pixel Help. Search your Settings for "Magic Cue."

## Samsung Galaxy: Galaxy AI

Samsung's current help pages put Galaxy AI settings under **Settings > Galaxy AI** ([Samsung UK](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/how-to-use-galaxy-ai-features-on-photo-assist/), updated 2026-01-30).

### Keep it on the phone

Samsung says "You can decide where your Galaxy AI data gets processed," either on the device or in the cloud ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/galaxy-ai/)). The setting that matters is **Process data only on device**.

The only path we could confirm is from a 2024 page for the Galaxy S24: Settings > Advanced features > Advanced intelligence > Samsung keyboard > Style and grammar, turn on Process data only on device ([Samsung UK](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/how-to-use-the-galaxy-ai-features-on-the-s24-series/), updated 2024-04-29). Samsung's current Galaxy AI page only says to "Go to your privacy settings" ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/galaxy-ai/)).

### Personal data intelligence

On the Galaxy S26, Settings > Galaxy AI > Now nudge has a switch for Now nudge itself, which "recognises what is shown on your screen" ([Samsung AU](https://www.samsung.com/au/support/mobile-devices/how-to-use-the-now-nudge-feature-on-the-samsung-galaxy-s26-series/)). On the same page, Personal data intelligence controls Samsung's analysis of your personal data. Samsung says the data "is processed on your phone unless you consent to share it with Samsung or its partners" ([Samsung AU](https://www.samsung.com/au/support/mobile-devices/how-to-use-the-now-nudge-feature-on-the-samsung-galaxy-s26-series/)), and that "Analyzed data will be deleted once the Personal Data Intelligence menu is turned off" ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/galaxy-ai/)).

### Gemini and Perplexity on the side button

On the Galaxy S25, Samsung said "Just press and hold the side button to activate Gemini" ([Samsung](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-galaxy-s25-series-sets-the-standard-of-ai-phone-as-a-true-ai-companion)). That means the Gemini settings in the Android section apply to Galaxy phones too, including Connected Apps.

Perplexity on the S26 is opt-in, and setting it up makes it your default digital assistant. To undo it: change the side button under Settings > Advanced features > Side button > Long press, turn off Hey Perplexity detection under Settings > Advanced features > Voice wake-up, and pick a different Digital assistant app under Settings > Apps > Default apps ([Samsung UK](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/how-to-use-perplexity-on-the-samsung-galaxy-s26-series/)).

## Apps that brought their own AI

### Meta AI in WhatsApp

WhatsApp calls Meta AI "optional," but its help pages describe no way to remove or hide it ([WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/2257017191175152)). We found none.

WhatsApp says "Only messages that mention @Meta AI, or that people choose to share with Meta AI, can be read by Meta," and Meta may use them "to improve AI quality." You can delete chats with Meta AI. To delete Meta AI's copy of what you shared, type /reset-ai in a chat, or /reset-all-ais for all chats ([WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/688464770796037)).

The closest thing to an off switch works one chat at a time. **Restricted chat** is replacing Advanced Chat Privacy. When it's on, nobody in the chat can use Meta AI in it, export it, or open it on linked devices. In groups, only admins can turn it on ([WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/715385484388016)). Chats that already had Advanced Chat Privacy "will not update to Restricted chat automatically," so turn it on again in each one, from your main phone. Advanced Chat Privacy was under the chat name ([WhatsApp](https://blog.whatsapp.com/introducing-advanced-chat-privacy)); we couldn't confirm Restricted chat sits in the same place.

### Meta AI chats now shape your ads

Since December 16, 2025, in most regions, Meta uses what you say to Meta AI to personalize the posts and ads you see across its apps ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/improving-your-recommendations-apps-ai-meta/), post dated 2025-10-01). Meta says it won't use topics like health, religion and politics to show you ads. It counts WhatsApp chats only if WhatsApp is in your Meta Accounts Center, which WhatsApp says is being renamed Meta Account ([WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/2257017191175152)). Meta describes no full opt-out. The one sure control is not using Meta AI.

We could not confirm whether Meta AI can be turned off or hidden in Instagram or Messenger. Meta's page on the subject didn't show the detail when we read it ([Meta](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/genai/)).

### Gmail smart features

Gmail's **smart features** include Smart Compose, inbox categories and Gemini summaries. Google says they are off by default only in the European Economic Area, Japan, Switzerland and the UK, and that "When smart features are on, your data may be used to improve these features" ([Google](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/10079371?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid), read 2026-10-10).

There are three boxes. In the Gmail app on Android:

1. Menu > Settings > pick your account > General, uncheck **Smart features**.
2. Menu > Settings > pick your account > General > Google Workspace smart features, uncheck **Smart features in Google Workspace** and **Smart features in other Google products**.

Google says the change applies across your devices, but also that some changes made on a computer don't show up in the phone app, so check the app too.

**Gemini in Gmail** (the "Ask Gemini" button, which also drafts emails) is a separate thing. For personal accounts, Google says it "requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan" ([Google](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14199860?hl=en)). The only other route Google names is its Workspace Experiments tester program.

### Microsoft Copilot app

Microsoft says an updated version of the Copilot app for web, desktop and mobile has been available since August 18, 2026, and its documents differ from the older app's ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/microsoft-copilot/privacy-controls)).

- **New app:** Microsoft says prompts, responses and file contents "aren't used to train foundation models" ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/microsoft-copilot/activity-history)). Under Settings > Personalization you can turn off Saved memories, One shared experience (sharing with Bing, Edge and MSN) and Allow ads personalization ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/microsoft-copilot/privacy-controls)).
- **Older app, if you haven't updated:** Microsoft's privacy FAQ, which says it covers the app before the August 2026 update, says conversations are used to train its AI models by default for signed-in adults in most countries, and are kept for 18 months by default ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/topic/privacy-faq-for-microsoft-copilot-27b3a435-8dc9-4b55-9a4b-58eeb9647a7f)). To opt out in the mobile app: open the menu, tap your profile icon, then Account > Privacy, and turn off Training on conversation activity and Training on voice conversations ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/microsoft-copilot-privacy-controls)).

## FAQ

**How do I turn off Apple Intelligence?**

On iOS 26, go to Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri and turn off Apple Intelligence ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/intro-to-apple-intelligence-iphc28624b81/26/ios/26)). On iOS 27, Apple's guide lists only per-feature switches ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-iph3fed3f2c3/ios)), so work through the list in the iPhone section.

**Does turning off Gemini Apps Activity disconnect Gemini from my calls and messages?**

No. Google says Phone, Messages, WhatsApp and Device assistance stay available "whether Keep Activity is on or off" ([Google](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en)). Turn each off under Connected Apps in the Gemini app.

**Can I remove Meta AI from WhatsApp?**

Not that we could find. WhatsApp's help pages describe no way to remove it ([WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/2257017191175152)). Restricted chat stops anyone using Meta AI in a given chat, and you can delete your Meta AI chats ([WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/715385484388016)).

**Does Apple use my data to train AI?**

Apple says data sent to Private Cloud Compute "is not stored or made accessible to Apple" ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-intelligence-and-privacy-iphe3f499e0e/ios)). Separately, if you opted in to Improve Siri & Dictation, Apple keeps audio and transcripts for up to two years and employees review some of it ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/improve-siri-dictation/)).

**Is on-device AI private?**

When processing really stays on the phone, your data never reaches a server. Google says Pixel's Scam Detection works this way ([Google](https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/15654065?hl=en)). But many features mix phone and cloud, as Magic Cue shows ([Google Store](https://store.google.com/intl/en/ideas/articles/magic-cue/)), so check the company's page for each one.

**Will updates turn AI back on?**

Expect it. Apple turned Apple Intelligence on automatically in iOS 18.3 ([Apple developer release notes](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/ios-ipados-release-notes/ios-ipados-18_3-release-notes.json)) and again made it "automatically available" in iOS 27 ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)). After each big update, recheck the settings in this guide.
