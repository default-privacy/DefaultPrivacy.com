The easiest second number comes from a phone-number app such as [MySudo](/directory/mysudo) or [JMP](/directory/jmp-chat): you pay a few dollars a month and give that number out instead of your own. If you need it for verification codes, a number on a second prepaid SIM or eSIM is less likely to be refused, because some services reject internet-based (VoIP) numbers. Either way the provider usually knows who pays, so a second number keeps your real one away from shops, strangers and data brokers without making you anonymous.

On the former Techlore privacy forum, signing up for things without a real phone number was among the most-viewed questions, and a quarter of the help requests about phone numbers got one reply or none.

---

## Why keep your real number to yourself

A phone number works as a lookup key. In a 2021 Princeton study, 171 of 259 numbers that two US carriers listed as available on their online number-change pages brought up the previous owner's details on people-search sites ([Princeton](https://recyclednumbers.cs.princeton.edu/)). Companies match numbers too. The FTC says Twitter let advertisers target people by matching the phone numbers users gave for account security against data the advertisers already had or bought from data brokers ([FTC, May 2022](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/05/ftc-charges-twitter-deceptively-using-account-security-data-sell-targeted-ads)).

A second number is worth having wherever a stranger or a company asks for your number and doesn't need the real one:

- **Sign-ups, loyalty programs and checkouts.** Every company that gets the second number is one fewer holding your real one.
- **Selling things online.** Buyers you've never met get a number you can drop after the sale. If a buyer asks you to read back a code they've just texted you, don't. Google says never to share a Google Voice verification code with anyone, and you can [disconnect your number](https://voice.google.com/regain) from Google Voice if someone tricked you into it ([Google Voice Help](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9177797?hl=en)).
- **Dating.** A match can call or text you without getting the number your bank, your family and people-search sites know.
- **Data brokers.** To clear the copies of your real number already out there, see [Remove your name from data brokers](/guides/remove-name-from-data-brokers).

## Option 1: a phone-number app

These apps give you a number that rings and texts inside the app, over Wi-Fi or mobile data. They are VoIP numbers, meaning the calls travel over the internet ([JMP FAQ](https://jmp.chat/faq)).

- **[MySudo](/directory/mysudo)** needs a paid plan for phone numbers. The free plan has email addresses and in-app messaging but no number. SudoGo, $1.99 a month in the US, includes one number with 30 minutes and 100 messages a month, and SudoMax includes nine numbers ([MySudo plans](https://mysudo.com/mysudo-plans/)). Each number goes in a profile called a Sudo. The included numbers are a lifetime total: a number you delete or change still counts, and extras cost $0.99 each ([MySudo Support](https://support.mysudo.com/hc/en-us/articles/6764330466203--How-many-phone-numbers-do-I-get)). UK numbers require identity verification ([MySudo plans](https://mysudo.com/mysudo-plans/)).
- **[JMP](/directory/jmp-chat)** costs $4.99 a month for a US or Canadian number, plus $2.45 a month for each extra line ([JMP FAQ](https://jmp.chat/faq)). It runs on Jabber (XMPP), an open messaging network, so you use an app such as Cheogram on Android, Conversations, Snikket or [Movim](/directory/movim). A new account often has to receive a text from a person before it can send messages or place calls ([JMP](https://jmp.chat/)).
- **[Hushed](/directory/hushed)** offers US, Canadian and UK numbers in more than 300 area codes, with plans from $6.99 a month ([Hushed pricing](https://hushed.com/pricing/)).
- **[Burner](/directory/burner-app)** is an app, despite the name. Its Standard plan is $47.99 a year on its website, and you can delete a number and switch to a new one ([Burner pricing](https://www.burnerapp.com/pricing)).
- **[Cloaked](/directory/cloaked)** creates a separate phone number and email alias for each account you sign up for ([Cloaked](https://www.cloaked.com/features/identities)), bundled with data-broker removal, from $14.99 a month or $119.99 a year ([Cloaked pricing](https://www.cloaked.com/pricing)).
- **[MoneroSMS](/directory/monerosms)** sells a US number for texts only at $3.60 a month plus a charge per message, and says it can't always receive account activation codes ([MoneroSMS](https://monerosms.com/)).

The provider usually knows who pays, and if you subscribe through the App Store or Google Play, that store manages the subscription on your account ([Burner pricing](https://www.burnerapp.com/pricing)).

Keep your main phone for emergencies. JMP, for example, says it does not provide 911, 112, 999 or other emergency services ([JMP](https://jmp.chat/)).

### Google Voice: free, with trade-offs

[Google Voice](https://voice.google.com/) gives you a free US number for calls, texts and voicemail ([Google Voice Help](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/115061?hl=en)). What it costs you instead:

- **US only.** The no-charge plan is for personal use and is only available in the US ([Google Voice Help](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9249103?hl=en)).
- **It's linked to you.** The free plan needs a personal Google account and verification with another phone number ([Google Voice Help](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9249103?hl=en)), so Google holds your other number alongside the Voice number.
- **Google keeps the records.** Google says Voice saves and processes your call, text and voicemail information ([Google Voice Help](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/115061?hl=en)).
- **Use it or lose it.** Google may reclaim the number if you don't place calls or send texts for 3 months ([Google Voice policies](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9230450?hl=en)).
- **Services can spot it.** Twilio, which sells number lookups to businesses, classes Google Voice numbers as "non-fixed VoIP": numbers obtained online without a physical device ([Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/docs/lookup/v2-api/line-type-intelligence)).

## Option 2: a second prepaid SIM or eSIM

Many phones hold two numbers. iPhones can keep two active numbers using a physical SIM and an eSIM, or two eSIMs on iPhone 13 and later. To use two carriers, the iPhone must be unlocked by your carrier, or both plans must come from the same carrier ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)). Pixel 3a and later Pixels can use a physical SIM and an eSIM. In the US, Pixel 10 and later, except the Pixel 10 Pro Fold, take eSIMs only, and two eSIMs need a Pixel 7 or later and a carrier that allows it ([Pixel Phone Help](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/9449293?hl=en)). Check that your phone and carrier support a second line before you buy.

A cheap prepaid plan on the second line gives you a regular mobile number. Number sellers say services are less likely to refuse it than an app number ([Crypton](https://crypton.sh/); [MoneroSMS](https://monerosms.com/)).

**ID rules vary by country.** In February 2021 the GSMA, the mobile industry's trade body, counted 157 countries where prepaid SIM registration is mandatory ([GSMA, 2021](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Digital-Identity-Access-to-Mobile-Services-and-Proof-of-Identity-2021_SPREADs.pdf)). Two examples:

- **Germany.** Prepaid mobile providers must check your details against an ID card, passport or another approved method before they switch the service on ([§ 172 TKG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tkg_2021/__172.html)).
- **Australia.** Apart from some exemptions, a provider must not activate a prepaid mobile service until it has collected your details and verified your identity ([Identity Checks for Prepaid Mobile Carriage Services Determination 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2017L00399/latest/text)).

Check the rules where you live before you buy.

**Other routes to a SIM-based number:**

- **[Crypton.sh](/directory/crypton-sh)** rents numbers on physical SIM cards that it hosts, and you read the texts in its app or dashboard. Short rentals run 1 to 6 months from €14 a month, and in October 2026 the numbers on offer were UK, Dutch, Polish and Ukrainian ([Crypton](https://crypton.sh/)).
- **[Silent Link](/directory/silent-link)** sells eSIMs that work in more than 160 countries. Its data plan has no calls or texts. Its US plan, $98 a year, adds a US number that can receive texts but can't send them or make calls. Silent Link asks for no personal details, not even an email address ([Silent Link](https://silent.link/)). A provider that asks for no ID doesn't change the rules where you live.

Two numbers in one phone still share one device. The EFF notes that a mobile network can see which SIM cards have been used in which device ([EFF](https://ssd.eff.org/module/problem-mobile-phones)). A second SIM keeps your main number from the people you give the second one to. It doesn't hide the link from the carriers.

## Option 3: a burner phone

A burner is a cheap phone with its own prepaid SIM, kept for one purpose. It keeps the second number and its apps off your main phone entirely.

It isn't anonymous. The SIM falls under the same ID rules as any other. The EFF also points out that phones carried together or used in the same places can be linked to the same person, and that calling the same people from a new number gives the new number away ([EFF](https://ssd.eff.org/module/problem-mobile-phones)).

## Which numbers fail for verification codes

Services can look up what kind of line a number is ([Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/docs/lookup/v2-api/line-type-intelligence)). MySudo says some services don't accept VoIP numbers for sign-ups, messages or verification codes, and that each service sets its own rules ([MySudo Support](https://support.mysudo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019983354-Porting-a-Phone-Number-Into-MySudo)). MoneroSMS says virtual numbers are frequently blacklisted, which often breaks account activation ([MoneroSMS](https://monerosms.com/)).

In practice:

- **App numbers** (MySudo, JMP, Google Voice, Hushed, Burner, Cloaked, MoneroSMS) can fail wherever a service checks the line type.
- **SIM numbers** (a second prepaid SIM, a burner or a hosted SIM) are regular mobile numbers and less likely to be refused. Crypton, which sells both kinds, says its virtual numbers don't work for two-factor or app verification and points buyers to its SIM numbers instead ([Crypton](https://crypton.sh/)).
- **If a code doesn't arrive,** MySudo suggests asking the service for the code by email or phone call, or verifying with another number and switching to the Sudo number afterwards ([MySudo Support](https://support.mysudo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019981814-SMS-Verification-Codes-Short-Codes)). The second route leaves that other number on file with the service.
- **Skip one-time verification numbers** for any account you plan to keep. They're rented for a single code (Crypton's run for a 30-minute session) ([Crypton](https://crypton.sh/)), and the number then goes to someone else. Whoever holds a number gets its password-reset texts: Princeton researchers describe account takeovers through recycled numbers this way ([Princeton](https://recyclednumbers.cs.princeton.edu/)).

**For two-factor login, leave the phone number out.** CISA, the US cybersecurity agency, advises against SMS as a second factor because texts aren't encrypted, and rates codes from an authenticator app as better. Adding an authenticator app doesn't remove SMS, so CISA says to switch SMS off for each account once the app is set up. It also warns that some services may still use SMS for account recovery, so keep the number on those accounts working ([CISA, updated November 2025](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/guidance-mobile-communications-best-practices_508c.pdf)). [Aegis Authenticator](/directory/aegis-authenticator), [2FAS](/directory/2fas) and [Ente Auth](/directory/ente-auth) generate those codes.

**Don't let the number lapse.** JMP reclaims a number 30 days after you cancel unless you move it to another provider ([JMP FAQ](https://jmp.chat/faq)). MoneroSMS permanently revokes a number when the account runs out of funds ([MoneroSMS](https://monerosms.com/)). About 35 million US numbers are disconnected each year and routinely reassigned, according to the 2021 Princeton study ([Princeton](https://recyclednumbers.cs.princeton.edu/)), and the FCC runs a database so callers can check whether a number has changed hands ([FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/reassigned-numbers-database)). Move your accounts off a second number before you give it up.

## Keeping it separate

A second number only protects you while it stays apart from your real one.

- **Use it in place of your real number.** A company that has both can match them, as the FTC's Twitter case shows ([FTC, May 2022](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/05/ftc-charges-twitter-deceptively-using-account-security-data-sell-targeted-ads)).
- **Use one number per purpose** if your plan allows more than one. JMP pitches separate numbers for friends, dates and professional contacts ([JMP](https://jmp.chat/)).
- **Hide your number in messaging apps.** [Signal](/directory/signal) still needs a phone number to register, but since February 2024 you can chat by username. Setting Settings > Privacy > Phone Number > Who can find me by my number to "Nobody" stops people who have your number from finding you on Signal ([Signal](https://signal.org/blog/phone-number-privacy-usernames/)).
- **Lock a SIM-based number.** Most carriers let you set a PIN or passcode that is required before your number can be moved to another carrier, which helps block SIM-swap attacks ([CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/guidance-mobile-communications-best-practices_508c.pdf)).

## The legal line

Keeping your real number private is an ordinary reason to have a second one. Apple's own Dual SIM guide suggests one number for business and another for personal calls ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)).

Where it stops:

- **Fraud, harassment and impersonation.** Under the Truth in Caller ID Act, FCC rules prohibit faking caller ID with intent to defraud, cause harm or wrongly obtain anything of value, with penalties of up to $10,000 per violation ([FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/spoofing)). Google Voice's rules forbid impersonating anyone, harassment and creating accounts under false or fraudulent pretenses ([Google Voice policies](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9230450?hl=en)).
- **ID checks.** Where the law requires ID for a SIM, as in Germany and Australia, give your real details.
- **People with a legal right to reach you.** A second number isn't a way to dodge a court, a creditor or anyone else entitled to contact you. Give them the number you actually answer.

## FAQ

**Can I get a second phone number for free?**

In the US, Google Voice's basic plan costs nothing, but it needs a personal Google account and verification with another phone number ([Google Voice Help](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9249103?hl=en)). MySudo's free plan doesn't include a phone number ([MySudo plans](https://mysudo.com/mysudo-plans/)). The other options here charge a monthly or yearly fee.

**Will a second number work for verification codes?**

Sometimes. Services can tell an app number from a mobile one ([Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/docs/lookup/v2-api/line-type-intelligence)), and some refuse app numbers ([MySudo Support](https://support.mysudo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019983354-Porting-a-Phone-Number-Into-MySudo)). A prepaid SIM number is less likely to be refused. For two-factor login, an authenticator app is the better choice ([CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/guidance-mobile-communications-best-practices_508c.pdf)).

**Do I have to give Signal my real number?**

You need a phone number to register, but you don't have to share it. Chat by username, and set Who can find me by my number to "Nobody" ([Signal, February 2024](https://signal.org/blog/phone-number-privacy-usernames/)).

**Do I need ID to buy a prepaid SIM?**

It depends on the country. Germany and Australia require the provider to check your identity before the service is switched on ([§ 172 TKG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tkg_2021/__172.html); [Determination 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2017L00399/latest/text)), and in 2021 the GSMA counted 157 countries with mandatory prepaid SIM registration ([GSMA](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Digital-Identity-Access-to-Mobile-Services-and-Proof-of-Identity-2021_SPREADs.pdf)).

**Is a burner phone anonymous?**

No. The SIM falls under the same ID rules as any other, and networks can link phones that travel together or call the same people ([EFF](https://ssd.eff.org/module/problem-mobile-phones)). What a burner does is keep the second number and its apps off your main phone.

**Is it legal to have a second phone number?**

Having one to keep your real number private is ordinary, and phones support it out of the box ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)). In the US, faking caller ID to defraud or harm someone is illegal ([FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/spoofing)). Where your country requires ID for a SIM, the provider will ask for it.

**What happens to my second number if I stop paying?**

It goes back to the provider and can be given to someone else. JMP reclaims a cancelled number after 30 days ([JMP FAQ](https://jmp.chat/faq)), and Google can reclaim a Voice number after 3 months without calls or texts ([Google Voice policies](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9230450?hl=en)). Move any accounts off the number first.

**Can I make a Discord account without my real phone number?**

Usually, yes. On a computer or in a browser, Discord's sign-up asks for an email, username, password and date of birth, not a phone number ([Discord Support](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/31676852332439-Discord-Sign-Up-and-Registration-Guide)). Discord may ask you to verify a phone later, for example when a server requires it or after activity such as joining many servers in a short time, and it says "VOIP, Burner, or Landline numbers cannot be used" ([Discord Support](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/6181726888215-How-to-Verify-Your-Discord-Account)). Servers set to the Highest verification level require a verified phone ([Discord Support](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/216679607-Verification-Levels)). Since the phone-number apps above give VoIP numbers, a second prepaid SIM or eSIM is the option to try if Discord asks, though Discord doesn't say which mobile numbers it accepts.
