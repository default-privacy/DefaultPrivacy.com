Financial freedom in the privacy context isn't about wealth — it's about the ability to use your money without permission or surveillance.

## Threats to Financial Freedom

- **Account freezing**: Banks and payment processors can freeze funds without court order
- **Deplatforming**: PayPal, Stripe, banks can close accounts based on political or social pressure
- **Surveillance**: Every card transaction is tracked and profiled
- **CBDCs**: Programmable money that can restrict what you buy
- **KYC everywhere**: Identity verification required for increasingly small transactions

## The Principle

Financial privacy is not about hiding illegal activity. It's about maintaining the ability to donate to causes, buy products, and support businesses without every transaction being profiled, judged, and potentially used against you.
