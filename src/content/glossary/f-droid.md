F-Droid is the app store of choice for privacy-focused Android users.

## Key Features

- **All apps are open source**: Source code is available for review
- **No tracking**: No advertising ID, no analytics in the store itself
- **Anti-feature flags**: Warns about apps that track, show ads, or depend on proprietary services
- **Reproducible builds**: Verify that the APK matches the source code
- **No account required**: Browse and install without signing in

## Popular Apps Available

- **Aegis**: TOTP authenticator
- **NewPipe**: YouTube client without ads or tracking
- **Organic Maps**: Offline maps without tracking
- **Termux**: Terminal emulator
- **KeePassDX**: Password manager

## Limitations

- Smaller app selection than Google Play
- Some apps update more slowly (build and verification process)
- UI is more utilitarian than Google Play
