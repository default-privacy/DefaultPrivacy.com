Privacy coins implement privacy at the protocol level, making private transactions the default rather than an option.

## Major Privacy Coins

- **Monero (XMR)**: Ring signatures, stealth addresses, RingCT. The most established privacy coin.
- **Zcash (ZEC)**: Zero-knowledge proofs (zk-SNARKs). Privacy is optional (shielded transactions).
- **Pirate Chain (ARRR)**: Mandatory shielded transactions using Zcash technology.

## Why They Matter

- Bitcoin and Ethereum are pseudonymous, not anonymous — transactions are publicly traceable
- Chain analysis firms routinely de-anonymize Bitcoin users
- Privacy coins make surveillance of transactions infeasible

## Regulatory Pressure

- Several exchanges have delisted privacy coins under regulatory pressure
- Some countries have banned them
- Privacy advocates argue they're essential for financial freedom
- The tension between surveillance and privacy remains unresolved
