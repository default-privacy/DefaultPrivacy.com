Google is the largest data collection operation in history. Replacing Google services is one of the highest-impact privacy improvements you can make.

## Why Replace Google

- Google tracks you across **90%+ of websites** via Google Analytics, Ads, Fonts, reCAPTCHA
- **Gmail** scans emails for ad targeting and AI training
- **Chrome** sends browsing data to Google (even in "incognito" mode)
- **Google Search** builds a profile from every query you make
- **Google Maps** tracks your location history continuously
- **Android** phones send location data to Google ~340 times per day
- **YouTube** builds detailed interest profiles from viewing habits

## The Replacement Stack

### Search
| Google | Private Alternative | Notes |
|--------|-------------------|-------|
| Google Search | **Brave Search** | Independent index, no tracking |
| Google Search | **DuckDuckGo** | Doesn't log searches |
| Google Search | **Startpage** | Google results without the tracking |
| Google Search | **Kagi** | Paid, ad-free, excellent results |

### Email
| Google | Private Alternative | Notes |

## Migration Strategy

Don't try to replace everything at once. Priority order:

1. **Search engine** — Easiest switch, biggest daily impact
2. **Browser** — Stop feeding Chrome data to Google
3. **Email** — Hardest switch (changing email addresses), biggest long-term impact
4. **Phone** — GrapheneOS or CalyxOS for de-Googled Android
5. **Cloud storage** — Migrate files to encrypted alternatives
6. **Everything else** — Maps, calendar, docs, translate

## The Hard Truth

Complete de-Googling is difficult because Google's infrastructure is embedded in the web (analytics, fonts, CDNs, reCAPTCHA). But replacing the products you use directly eliminates the majority of data Google collects about you personally.
