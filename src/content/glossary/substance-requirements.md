Substance requirements are the world's answer to mailbox companies and paper residencies. Governments increasingly demand proof that you actually live, work, or operate where you claim to.

## Why Substance Matters

- **Tax authorities** check whether your claimed tax residency is genuine
- **Banks** verify that your business address isn't just a virtual mailbox
- **Immigration** monitors whether you actually spend time in the country that gave you residency
- **Treaty benefits** (tax treaties, FEIE) require genuine ties to a jurisdiction

## Types of Substance Requirements

### Personal Tax Residency Substance
Most countries require **183 days** of physical presence per year to claim tax residency. Some require less, some more:

| Country | Days Required | Additional Requirements |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| UAE | 90-183 days | Emirates ID, bank account, lease |
| Panama | 0 formal days | But immigration expects genuine ties |
| Georgia | 183 days | Simple registration process |
| Paraguay | 0 formal, but 183 for tax cert | Cedula, local address |

### Corporate Substance
For businesses, substance typically means:

- **Physical office** (not just a virtual address)
- **Local employees** or contractors
- **Local bank account** with real transactions
- **Board meetings** held in the jurisdiction
- **Decision-making** that demonstrably happens locally
- **Revenue generation** tied to local activities

### The "Mind and Management" Test
Many jurisdictions determine a company's tax residency by where its "mind and management" reside — meaning where key decisions are actually made. If you incorporate in Dubai but run everything from your laptop in Lisbon, the company may be deemed Portuguese for tax purposes.

## Common Substance Traps for Nomads

1. **Claiming UAE residency but spending 10 months in Europe**: Tax authorities in your actual location may claim you
2. **A company on paper in a place you never work**: The filing does not move your tax home
3. **Multiple residencies**: If two countries both consider you a tax resident, tax treaty tie-breaker rules apply
4. **Digital nomad visa ≠ tax residency**: Many nomad visas explicitly state you remain tax resident elsewhere

## How to Build Genuine Substance

- **Get a real lease** (not just an Airbnb)
- **Open local bank accounts** and use them regularly
- **Join local organizations** or coworking spaces
- **Keep travel records** meticulously (flights, stamps, boarding passes)
- **Register for local utilities** in your name
- **Maintain a local phone number**
- **File local tax returns** even if you owe zero tax

## The OECD/BEPS Impact

The OECD's Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS) initiative has pushed countries worldwide to implement economic substance laws. This means:

- Shell companies with no real activity face penalties or dissolution
- Jurisdictions that were previously "no questions asked" now require substance declarations
- Information exchange between countries makes it easier to detect lack of substance
