While primarily a copyright law, the DMCA has significant implications for privacy and security.

## Section 1201: Anti-Circumvention

- Makes it illegal to bypass digital rights management (DRM)
- This affects security researchers who need to examine software
- Repairing your own devices can technically violate the DMCA
- Exemptions are granted every 3 years but are narrow

## Section 512: Safe Harbor

- Protects platforms from liability for user-uploaded content
- Requires "notice and takedown" procedures
- Frequently abused for censorship (false takedown notices)

## Privacy Impact

- Anti-circumvention provisions can prevent auditing software for privacy violations
- DRM in IoT devices prevents users from examining what data is collected
- Security researchers risk legal action for disclosing vulnerabilities
- The law prioritizes copyright holder control over user privacy and security
