Sideloading lets you install apps without going through Google Play or the Apple App Store.

## Privacy Benefits

- Avoid app store tracking and profiling
- Install open-source apps directly from developers (F-Droid)
- Use apps that have been removed from official stores
- Avoid mandatory app store account requirements

## Risks

- No app store review process to catch malware
- Must trust the source of the APK/IPA file
- Auto-updates may not be available
- Sideloaded apps might not receive security patches promptly

## On iOS

Apple heavily restricts sideloading. The EU Digital Markets Act now requires Apple to allow alternative app stores in the EU.

For what to use, see [Apps without the Google Play Store](/guides/apps-without-the-google-play-store).
