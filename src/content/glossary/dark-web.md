The dark web is a small subset of the deep web (unindexed internet) that requires specific software to access.

## Size

- The surface web (Google-indexed): ~5% of the internet
- The deep web (databases, private pages): ~90%
- The dark web (Tor hidden services): <0.01% but culturally outsized

## Legitimate Uses

- Whistleblowing platforms (SecureDrop)
- News organizations (NYT, BBC, ProPublica onion sites)
- Communication in censored countries
- Privacy-focused email and messaging

## Criminal Uses

- Drug markets, fraud, stolen data
- These get the headlines but represent a minority of hidden services

## For Privacy

The dark web's privacy infrastructure (Tor, onion services) is the same technology that protects journalists, activists, and ordinary people who need privacy. The technology is neutral — only the use determines legality.
