Compartmentalization is the foundation of good operational security. By isolating different parts of your life, you limit the damage from any single breach.

## Digital Compartmentalization

- **Separate email addresses**: One for banking, one for shopping, one for social media
- **Multiple browsers**: Firefox for personal, Brave for work, Tor for sensitive browsing
- **Virtual machines**: Isolate different activities in separate VMs
- **Separate devices**: Dedicated phone for sensitive communications

## Identity Compartmentalization

- Real name for legal/financial matters
- Pseudonym for online activity
- Business entity (LLC) for commercial activity
- Different usernames per platform

## Why It Works

- A data breach at a shopping site doesn't expose your banking email
- A compromised social media account doesn't lead to your real identity
- Surveillance of one compartment doesn't reveal the others

## The Cost

Compartmentalization adds friction and complexity. The right level depends on your threat model. Most people need some compartmentalization; few need total separation.
