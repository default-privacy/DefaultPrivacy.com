The parallel economy is built by people who want to transact, communicate, and operate outside the surveillance infrastructure of Big Tech and traditional finance.

## Components

- **Privacy cryptocurrency**: Coins designed to make transactions hard to trace
- **Decentralized exchanges**: Trade without KYC or identity verification
- **Privacy-first services**: Services built to collect little data, used instead of mainstream ad-funded ones
- **Self-hosted infrastructure**: Running your own email, cloud, communication servers
- **Cash transactions**: The original parallel economy
- **Barter and direct trade**: No financial intermediary needed

## Why People Build Parallel Systems

- Financial censorship (accounts frozen for political views)
- Surveillance concerns (every transaction tracked and profiled)
- Platform risk (businesses deplatformed without recourse)
- Privacy as a right (not wanting to participate in surveillance capitalism)

## The Infrastructure Gap
