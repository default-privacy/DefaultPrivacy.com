Privacy screen protectors are the simplest physical privacy tool — they make your screen invisible from side angles.

## How They Work

- Micro-louver technology blocks light at wide angles
- Screen appears black when viewed from the side (typically >30 degrees)
- Person directly in front sees the screen normally (slightly dimmer)

## When to Use

- Working in cafes, airports, or coworking spaces
- Using your phone on public transport
- Offices with open floor plans
- Anywhere sensitive information is displayed in public

## Limitations

- Reduces screen brightness by 30-50%
- Slightly reduces image clarity
- Doesn't protect against cameras positioned directly above or behind you
- Only protects visual privacy, not electronic surveillance
