Palantir Technologies is one of the most powerful surveillance technology companies in the world.

## Products

- **Palantir Gotham**: Used by intelligence agencies and law enforcement. Integrates data from multiple sources for analysis.
- **Palantir Foundry**: Enterprise data integration platform.
- **Palantir Apollo**: Continuous delivery and deployment.

## Government Contracts

- CIA, NSA, FBI, DHS, ICE, IRS, CDC
- UK NHS (COVID tracking)
- Multiple US police departments
- Military intelligence operations

## Privacy Concerns

- Integrates data from social media, financial records, phone records, and government databases
- Enables predictive policing (profiling people before crimes occur)
- Used by ICE for immigration enforcement and deportation
- Lack of transparency about capabilities and use
- Founded with CIA venture capital funding (In-Q-Tel)

## Why It Matters

Palantir represents the industrialization of surveillance — making it easy for any government agency to cross-reference massive datasets about citizens. Understanding what companies like Palantir do is essential for understanding why privacy tools exist.
