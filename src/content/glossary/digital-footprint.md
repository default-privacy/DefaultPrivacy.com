Your digital footprint is the sum of everything the internet knows about you. Most people's footprints are far larger than they realize.

## Active Footprint

Data you deliberately create:
- Social media posts and comments
- Online purchases and reviews
- Forum posts and blog comments
- Email messages
- Account registrations

## Passive Footprint

Data collected without your active participation:
- Websites visited (via cookies and trackers)
- Location history (via phone and apps)
- Device information (via browser fingerprinting)
- Shopping habits (via loyalty cards and payment methods)
- Communication patterns (via metadata)

## How Large Is Yours?

- Google stores every search you've ever made (unless you've opted out)
- Facebook has an average of 1.5GB of data per user
- Data brokers compile 1,500+ data points per person
- Your ISP logs every website you visit

## Reducing Your Footprint

1. **Audit**: Search your name, check data broker sites, download your data from Google/Facebook
2. **Delete**: Remove old accounts, opt out of data brokers, delete unused apps
3. **Minimize going forward**: Use privacy tools, limit what you share, use aliases
4. **Encrypt**: Use encrypted messaging, email, and cloud storage
5. **Scan your exposure**: Use tools like Default Privacy's browser exposure check to see what you're leaking
