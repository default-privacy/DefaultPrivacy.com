Blockchain analysis de-anonymizes users of transparent blockchains like Bitcoin and Ethereum.

## Techniques

- **Address clustering**: Linking multiple addresses to the same entity
- **Exchange identification**: Mapping known exchange addresses
- **Transaction graph analysis**: Following the flow of funds
- **Dust attacks**: Sending tiny amounts to addresses to link them
- **Timing analysis**: Correlating transactions with real-world events

## Defense

On transparent chains, CoinJoin and mixers provide limited protection but are increasingly scrutinized.
