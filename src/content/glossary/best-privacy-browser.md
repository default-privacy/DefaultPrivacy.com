Your browser is your primary interface with the web — choosing one that respects privacy makes the biggest single impact on your daily privacy.

## Browser Rankings (Privacy)

### Tier 1: Maximum Privacy
- **Tor Browser**: Routes all traffic through Tor, uniform fingerprint, blocks trackers. Slow but most private.
- **Mullvad Browser**: Tor Browser without Tor — designed for VPN users. Minimal fingerprint.

### Tier 2: Strong Privacy
- **Brave**: Built-in ad/tracker blocking, fingerprint randomization, IPFS support. Chromium-based.
- **Firefox** (hardened): With strict tracking protection, uBlock Origin, and about:config tweaks.
- **LibreWolf**: Pre-hardened Firefox fork with privacy defaults.

### Tier 3: Acceptable Privacy
- **Safari**: Decent tracking protection, intelligent tracking prevention. Apple ecosystem only.
- **Firefox** (default): Good but not great out of the box. Needs extensions.

### Avoid for Privacy
- **Google Chrome**: Extensive data collection, limited tracking protection
- **Microsoft Edge**: Telemetry, tracking, Microsoft account integration
- **Opera**: Chinese-owned since 2016, built-in VPN is a proxy

## Recommended Setup

Brave or Firefox + uBlock Origin for daily browsing. Tor Browser for anything sensitive. Multiple profiles for compartmentalization.
