Your browser is your primary interface with the web — choosing one that respects privacy makes the biggest single impact on your daily privacy.

## What Makes a Browser Private

- **Tracking prevention**: blocking third-party cookies, trackers, and cross-site identifiers.
- **Fingerprinting resistance**: making your browser look like many others, or randomizing the signals sites can read.
- **Data collection practices**: what the browser vendor itself collects through telemetry, sync, and account integration.
- **Network privacy**: whether traffic is routed through something like Tor, which hides your IP address from sites.
- **Compartmentalization**: separate profiles or containers keep activities from sharing cookies and history.

Browsers differ widely on these points, and defaults matter: most people never change them.
